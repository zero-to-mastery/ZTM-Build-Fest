# v0.40.2 — Clock & Archive Correctness

**Candidate:** `kin-v0.40.2` on `fix/kin-v0.40.2-clock-archive-correctness`, based on `kin-development` after PR #37. This is a narrow patch to the v0.40 portability and restore work. It adds no product capability and does not change archive formats or live authorization policy.

## Corrections

- **P-003:** Portable membership is a historical snapshot. Validation checks its shape, known status and kind values, bounded Temporary expiry, required/forbidden expiry fields, unique member IDs, and member count without comparing status against the local clock. Archive export and restore preserve server-authored `active`, `removed`, and `expired` values. Restore no longer promotes an `active` status to `expired` based on local wall time. Live access and trust continue to be decided by server authorization.
- **P-004:** `PairingService.leaveHousehold` now passes `this.now()` to `canParticipate`, matching the rest of the service. A Temporary member can leave before expiry under the injected clock; expired Temporary members remain unauthorized. Existing Adult and Limited behavior is unchanged.

## Compatibility and security

There is no archive schema, version, cryptographic envelope, or package-signature change. Existing KARC v1 framing and package-v1/package-v2 archives remain supported; legacy packages without portable membership remain valid. Membership status is descriptive archive data and cannot create an identity, trusted device, session, sync state, household permission, or key authority. Restoring a removed member does not reauthorize that member. Revocation and expiry remain enforced by the server. B-002 committed-rotation recovery for trusted recipients is unchanged. R-3's honest-relay assumption and compromised-relay limitation are unchanged.
