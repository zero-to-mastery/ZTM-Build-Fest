# v0.38.1 — Authenticated Member Self-Leave

**Status:** Implemented candidate on `kin-fast-track-v0.36-v0.38`.

## Shipped behavior

- Limited and Temporary members can now leave their own household membership after fresh passkey authentication, while removal of other members remains Adult-only.
- Session-only leave remains rejected. A cancelled or failed passkey ceremony leaves membership and local authority unchanged.
- Successful self-leave deactivates the member, revokes their devices and sessions, clears local authority, and runs the existing access-change/key-epoch handling. Removed members cannot sync afterward.
- Last-Adult protection is unchanged: an Adult cannot leave if only Limited and/or Temporary members remain.
- The self-leave confirmation uses member-neutral language.
