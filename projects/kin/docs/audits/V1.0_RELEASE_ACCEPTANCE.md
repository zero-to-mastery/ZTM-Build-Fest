## Status language

- **PASS** — you ran it in the stated environment and it worked. Say what you
  did and on what.
- **PARTIAL** — automation or a stand-in covered the mechanics, but not the
  real environment the row asks for.
- **NOT RUN** — no qualifying evidence. Not a pass.

Every row that isn't PASS needs an owner decision in the last column:
**accept** (becomes a documented known limitation) or **block**.
