# Code of Conduct

Kin welcomes thoughtful, respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and experiences. This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where contributors represent Kin.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and behavior; do not attack, demean, threaten, or harass people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and disagree without hostility.
- Keep criticism proportionate and focused on the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post real household messages, identifying details, credentials, private keys, or another person's sensitive information without permission.
- Follow Kin's principles: no relationship judging, blame, contribution scoring, or surveillance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized conduct, intimidation, doxxing, repeated personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Kin discussions must not be used to shame or rank household members.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a Kin maintainer privately through the contact method available on the maintainer's GitHub profile or use the hosting repository's private reporting option if available. Do not include sensitive household data in a public report. The maintainers will review reports, protect reporter privacy as reasonably possible, and take proportionate action, which may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces.

Kin is hosted inside the ZTM Build Fest repository. Host-level moderation tools and the people able to enforce repository-wide actions are controlled by that repository's maintainers. This document does not claim a staffed moderation team or response-time guarantee.
